About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at the University at Buffalo, advised by Prof. Bibhu Datta Sahoo, and expect to graduate in December 2027. My research is on analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits, with a focus on VCO-based analog computing: ring oscillators and memristors that solve systems of linear equations and emulate quantum algorithms on chip in 65-nm CMOS.
