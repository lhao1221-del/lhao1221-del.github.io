Research Projects

SUNY at Buffalo, NSF Research Assistant (Sep 2024 to present)

  • Ring oscillator and memristor linear-equation solver. An 8 × 8 linear-equation system based on VCOs and memristors, fabricated in TSMC 65-nm CMOS. Measured results: 4 × 4 matrix inversion with 8-bit accuracy, 2 μs solution latency, 0.4 mW power, and 0.0146 mm2 area.

  • 12-qubit quantum computing chip. A VCO and memristor based chip in TSMC 65-nm CMOS that runs Grover's search algorithm and the quantum Fourier transform. Simulated in Cadence Virtuoso; tape-out planned.

  • Ring-VCO-based integrator in TSMC 65-nm CMOS, fabricated.

  • Dynamic element matching DAC in TSMC 65-nm CMOS, simulated in Cadence Virtuoso and Simulink.

Silicon-Based Optoelectronic Interface Driver Chip for Optical Computing, Xi'an Jiaotong University (Dec 2022 to May 2024)

  • A silicon-based optoelectronic interface driver chip with 1 Gb/s input rate, fabricated in TSMC 180-nm CMOS, supporting up to 8-bit optoelectronic digital-to-analog conversion.

  • The chip includes an 8-channel Mach-Zehnder modulator (MZM) driver and 8-segment MZM optical paths.

Pulsed TOF LiDAR Front-End Receiver, Xi'an Jiaotong University (Sep 2021 to Nov 2022)

  • A foldable stacked in-channel structure achieves current reuse and higher power efficiency in TSMC 180-nm CMOS. The chip has been fabricated and is now in commercial production.

  • Fully integrates the low-power supply with the front-end circuit.
